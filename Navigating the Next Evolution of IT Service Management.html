Major Differences: v4 vs v5

Understanding the syllabus evolution and the shift in examination priorities.

Operational Shift

Practice procedures (Incident, Change) have been moved entirely to the Practice Manager modules, leaving Foundation to focus on Strategy.

Lifecycle Evolution

The 6-activity Service Value Chain (v4) is replaced by the 8-activity Product & Service Lifecycle (v5) for better alignment with DevOps.

Feature ITIL 4 Foundation ITIL Foundation v5
Core Focus IT Service Management Digital Product Management
AI Approach Emerging/Optional AI-Native Core
Exam Content 42% Procedure Memorization 0% Procedures / 70% Strategy
